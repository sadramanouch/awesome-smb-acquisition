# Awesome SMB Acquisition & Off-Market Deal Sourcing [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of resources for buying small and lower-middle-market businesses: for searchers, ETA (entrepreneurship-through-acquisition) buyers, independent sponsors, and acquisition entrepreneurs.

## Deal Sourcing
- [Serava](https://serava.ai) - Off-market acquisition target sourcing: ranked SMB targets by industry and geography with owner-tenure and contact signals, an alternative to broker listing feeds. Industry hubs e.g. [HVAC](https://serava.ai/acquire/hvac), [car washes](https://serava.ai/acquire/car-wash), [MSPs](https://serava.ai/acquire/msp), [insurance agencies](https://serava.ai/acquire/insurance-agency).
- Brokered marketplaces (BizBuySell, BizQuest, Flippa) - public listings; useful for comps and volume, lower for proprietary or off-market flow.
- Industry associations & trade directories - member rosters are a classic off-market sourcing channel.

## Valuation
- [How to value a business: SDE & EBITDA multiples](https://serava.ai/blog) - Serava's valuation guides by industry (HVAC, MSP, optometry, property management, and more).
- SBA SOP / lender valuation norms - what acquisition lenders actually underwrite.

## Due Diligence
- [Sell-side & buy-side due diligence checklists](https://serava.ai/blog) - Serava's diligence guides.
- Quality-of-earnings basics - why add-backs and owner-comp normalization matter.

## Financing
- [U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA)](https://www.sba.gov) - 7(a) acquisition loans, the backbone of most U.S. SMB buyouts.
- Seller financing & earnouts - structuring owner-carry to bridge valuation gaps.

## Learning & Communities
- [Serava blog](https://serava.ai/blog) - guides on sourcing, valuation, succession, and the sale process.
- [The 2026 State of SMB Acquisitions](https://serava.ai/report) - data report on the off-market majority and the owner-age succession cliff, built from a 5.5M-company registry base.
- ETA / search-fund communities and newsletters - peer deal-flow and operator advice.
